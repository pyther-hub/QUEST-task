−∇·(a∇u) = 1 on the unit square with u = 0 on the edge, on a 128×128 grid. Pick, draw or write a permeability a(x). The Fourier Neural Operator predicts the pressure u(x), and an exact finite-volume solve gives the reference.

A JavaScript expression in x (left → right) and y (bottom → top), both in [0, 1]. Math functions work without a prefix: sin, exp, hypot, PI, … With snap on, a true or > 0.5 result gives a = 1 and anything else gives a = 0.1. With snap off, values are clamped to [0.1, 1].

The model was trained on two-phase fields made from 5 smooth random blobs. Shapes outside that family (thin lines, checkerboards, smooth values between 0.1 and 1) are out of distribution, and the error panel shows how much that costs. Timings are measured on the server's CPU for one sample.
